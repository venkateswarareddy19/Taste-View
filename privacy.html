Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about how we use it.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Taste✦View website and related services. By using our platform, you agree to the practices described here. Questions? Contact hello@tasteview.in.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use Taste✦View:

  • Account information: When you register, we collect your name and email address. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt — never stored in plain text.
  • Favourites: A list of place IDs you have saved, stored locally in your browser's localStorage.
  • City preference: Your selected city, stored locally so the app remembers it between visits.
  • Trip plans: Saved trip plans are stored locally in your browser's localStorage only.
  • AI chat messages: Text you send to our AI assistant is processed by our server and sent to Anthropic's API. We do not permanently store your chat history.
  • Voice input: When you use the voice assistant, audio is processed locally by your browser's Web Speech API. No audio is sent to our servers.
  • Usage data: Standard server logs (IP address, page requests, timestamps) for security monitoring. Logs are retained for 30 days.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and personalise the Taste✦View experience
  • To authenticate your account securely
  • To process your queries through our AI assistant
  • To remember your city preference and favourites
  • To monitor for abuse, fraud, and security threats
  • To respond to your enquiries submitted via our contact form

We do not use your data for advertising, nor do we sell your information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Anthropic: Your AI chat messages are sent to Anthropic's Claude API for processing. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts & Font Awesome: Used to load fonts and icons. These services may log standard request headers.
  • Leaflet / OpenStreetMap: Used for the India map feature. Map tile requests may be logged by tile providers.
  • Unsplash: Some images are served from Unsplash CDN. They may log image requests.

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any advertising trackers.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

  • tv_favs_v1: IDs of places you have saved. Stored in your browser's localStorage.
  • tv_recently_viewed: Places you have recently viewed. Stored in localStorage.
  • tv_trip_plans: Your saved trip plans. Stored in localStorage.
  • tv_consent: Records that you accepted this cookie notice.
  • tv_session: An HttpOnly, Secure session cookie set after login. Cannot be read by JavaScript. Expires after 7 days.

We do not use tracking cookies. You may clear your browser's localStorage and cookies at any time to remove all locally stored data.

5. Security

  • All connections are encrypted via HTTPS (TLS 1.2+)
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (cost factor 12) — we never store plain-text passwords
  • Session tokens are stored in HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Strict cookies
  • Our AI proxy keeps your Anthropic API key server-side — never exposed to the browser
  • Login attempts are rate-limited to 5 per 15 minutes per IP address
  • Content Security Policy headers restrict which scripts and resources can be loaded

Despite our efforts, no system is perfectly secure. If you discover a vulnerability, please contact security@tasteview.in.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data via your Profile page or by emailing us.
  • Withdrawal of consent: You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser data or deleting your account.

To exercise any of these rights, contact hello@tasteview.in. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Taste✦View is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified via a notice on our website. Continued use of Taste✦View after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
